Behaviour
open
Click the field, or ↓ on it. ↑↓ move, Esc closes.
link
Pick a spacer. The field shows the spacer name on white and drops the PX chip: the token owns value and unit.
unlink
Type a number, or Detach Style. Both keep the resolved value.
unit
PX opens the unit list. Typing 1.5rem sets value and unit at once.
scrub
Hover the axis icon and the cursor turns into the resize arrow. Press and drag along that axis to nudge the value, Shift for steps of 10. A linked field turns custom and starts counting from the token’s value (Spacer 1 = 4 px).
individual
The right button expands the row in place: left and right in the first column, top and bottom in the second. The lock is not offered while it is open.
