Content Label

Source: Figma — Lexicon Satellites. Borderless tinted tag for content metadata (types, categories, collections…). Nine colour variants — Figma content-0…content-8 — built from the chart palette (-l5 fill + -d2 text; yellow uses -d4), 4px radius, so they reskin in Dark and High Contrast. Variants: --0 neutral · --1 purple · --2 yellow · --3 green · --4 red · --5 teal · --6 cyan · --7 orange · --8 pink.
