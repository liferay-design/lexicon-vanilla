Global Menu

Source: Figma — Lexicon Satellites › Global Menu (kit width 240px, 8px radius) · spec: Confluence PEDS "Global Menu". Hub to jump between products (product icons from product-icons/*-20.svg), admin tools and sites. Behaviour from navigation.js (opt-in): opens from the .global-menu-trigger, closes on Esc (focus returns to the trigger) or outside click, arrow keys move through items, and a global-menu:select event tells the page what was chosen. Mobile (<768px) adds the .global-menu-scrim overlay.
