Top Bar

App bar for the unified navigation: 56px (48px below 768px). .top-bar__start holds the Side Menu toggle and the title; .top-bar__end holds actions and the Global Menu trigger (it stays above the mobile overlay). Buttons are .top-bar__btn (32px, 8px radius). The toggle and the title react to the Side Menu state (navigation.js): the toggle swaps icon / brand and tooltip, the title shifts when the menu is pinned. See it live in prototypes/unified-navigation.html.

Sites

Toggle brand (shown while the menu peeks): .top-bar__brand--contain for a 20px product icon or vector logo, a plain .top-bar__brand for a site photo, .top-bar__brand--global for the Global glyph. Add data-follow-side-menu to .top-bar__title to make it follow the chosen Side Menu item.
